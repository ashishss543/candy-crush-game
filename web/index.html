CANDY MATCH SAGA

An Intelligent Rule-Based Candy Match Game with Player Analytics
How to play
  • Drag a candy onto a neighbour, or click one candy then a neighbour, to swap them.
  • A swap is valid when it makes a match of 2 or more. Invalid swaps bounce back and cost no move.
  • Match 2 is a pair combo, Match 3 is standard, Match 4 makes a Striped Candy (clears a line), Match 5 makes a Color Bomb (swap it with any candy to wipe that colour).
  • Chain cascades to raise your combo multiplier, up to 5x.
  • Open + Candy Synthesis to define formulas. Line up the ingredients next to each other to craft a new candy.
  • Open + Rule to add bonus rules. The AI Worth Check accepts or rejects anything that would break the balance.
  • Reach the target score before the moves run out.
